EXPLAINABILITY METHODOLOGICAL JUSTIFICATION

Deep Learning Model: PyTorch LSTM (24-hour lookback window)
Explainability Method: SHAP (TreeExplainer on Comparable Random Forest)

Rationale:
Multi-step 3D recurrent sequences (24 time steps x features) in an LSTM are
difficult to interpret directly because temporal feature attributions span
across lagged hidden states. In accordance with task guidelines, SHAP/LIME
attributions were computed on a comparable Random Forest regressor trained on
the exact same chronological forecast horizon using 1-hour, 2-hour, and 24-hour
traffic volume lags alongside exogenous time and weather features.
